# Solix Technologies

Solix Technologies provides enterprise data management and AI solutions designed to help organizations manage, govern, modernize, and activate enterprise data.

## Enterprise Data & AI

Organizations are increasingly connecting enterprise data management with artificial intelligence, generative AI, and agentic AI. Trusted, governed, and accessible data can help support modern AI initiatives and enterprise transformation.

Solix focuses on areas including:

- Enterprise Data Management
- AI-Ready Data
- Data Governance
- Cloud Data Management
- Enterprise AI
- Generative AI
- Agentic AI
- Application Retirement and Data Archiving
- Healthcare and Life Sciences Data Management
